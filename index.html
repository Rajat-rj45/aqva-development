Welcome

Welcome to Aqva Development

Nestled in the fast-developing corridor of Sohna Road, Aqva Development is an exclusive plotted development designed for those who value privacy, premium living, and long-term growth potential. Developed by Dreamways Infra Projects Private Limited, this boutique project stands out with its low-density layout, offering just 78 premium plots spread across 5.06 acres of thoughtfully planned land.

What truly sets Aqva Development apart is its focus on creating a peaceful and refined living environment. With limited inventory, residents enjoy enhanced privacy, less congestion, and a more community-driven lifestyle, something rarely found in today's high-density developments.

The project offers well-sized residential plots in 147, 172, and 178 sq. yards, catering to modern families looking to build their dream home exactly the way they envision it. Whether you're an end-user or an investor, these plot sizes strike the perfect balance between affordability and spacious living.

Adding to the premium experience is a massive 1.25 lakh sq. ft. clubhouse, designed to deliver a lifestyle that goes beyond just living. From recreational amenities to wellness and social spaces, Aqva Development ensures that every resident enjoys a holistic and elevated lifestyle within the community.

Strategically located in Sector 05, Sohna Road, the project benefits from excellent connectivity to major hubs of Gurgaon and Delhi NCR, while still offering a serene escape from the city's hustle. With rapid infrastructure development in the surrounding areas, Aqva Development also presents strong potential for future appreciation, making it a smart investment choice.

Aqva Development isn't just a plotted development. It's a rare opportunity to own a part of a thoughtfully crafted, low-density community where luxury meets tranquility.

About Aqva Development

Aqva Development is an exclusive low-density plotted development located in Sector 05, Sohna Road, Gurgaon, thoughtfully designed for those who value privacy, space, and long-term growth. Spread across 5.06 acres with just 78 premium plots, the project offers a rare opportunity to be part of a peaceful, well-planned community away from the congestion of high-rise living. Developed by Dreamways Infra Projects Private Limited, it features carefully sized plots of 147, 172, and 178 sq. yards, giving you the flexibility to design and build your dream home exactly the way you envision.
